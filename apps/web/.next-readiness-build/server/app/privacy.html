Privacy Policy

A complete privacy policy has not yet been supplied for publication. The information below describes the current product; it does not replace the final policy.

The website demonstration

The interactive message demonstration uses local sample rules in your browser. It does not send your entered text to an AI service or WhatsApp, and it does not save an order. Please use fictional details.

The business application

The application stores account and business information, customer records, conversations, products and orders for business operations. Authentication uses server-managed sessions. Access is scoped to the signed-in organization and assigned role.

Connected services

When configured, AI extraction can send message text to an AI provider. WhatsApp integration uses Meta services, and billing uses PayHere. These integrations require configuration and may be subject to their providers’ policies.

Information still to be published

The final policy must specify the responsible legal entity, retention periods, processing locations, applicable privacy rights and request procedures. These details are not asserted here. Contact the team before submitting sensitive customer information.
