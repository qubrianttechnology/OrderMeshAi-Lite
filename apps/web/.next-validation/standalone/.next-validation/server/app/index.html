Getting to know OrderMesh?

Who is OrderMesh for?+

OrderMesh AI Lite is designed for Sri Lankan wholesalers and distributors who need one place to manage customers, product stock and orders.

How does the order assistant work?+

Paste Sinhala or English text and review the extracted products and quantities. The default mode uses local catalog-matching rules. An optional external AI model can be configured by your operator. Matches and confidence flags always need your review; nothing becomes an order until you confirm.

Can I use it on my phone?+

Yes. The workspace adapts to mobile screens, so you can review orders, check customers and look at stock from your browser.

Does it connect to WhatsApp or accept voice notes?+

Not in this release. Start by pasting a text order. Official WhatsApp integration, voice notes and Tamil extraction are future extensions, not active features.

What happens when I get started?+

You’ll go to a separate signup page to create your account and organization. Then you can add customers, products and opening stock. You get a 15-day free trial; the Lite plan is LKR 7,400 per month. Subscription checkout is available when PayHere is configured.
